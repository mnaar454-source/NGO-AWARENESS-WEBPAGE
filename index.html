About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit organization committed to creating positive social change through education, skill development, environmental conservation, women empowerment, animal welfare, and community development. The foundation works with volunteers, partners, and local communities to improve the lives of underprivileged people across India.

Mission

To empower underprivileged communities by providing opportunities in education, healthcare, skill development, environmental sustainability, and social welfare.

Vision

To create an inclusive and sustainable society where every individual has equal opportunities to learn, grow, and lead a dignified life.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project SEVA

Providing food, clothing, and essential support to underprivileged individuals and families.

Every meal shared brings hope, dignity, and a brighter tomorrow for those in need.

Project BACHPANSHALA

Supporting children through education, digital literacy, and life skills.

Education empowers children to break the cycle of poverty and build a successful future.

Project JEEV

Caring for stray animals through food, shelter, and compassion.

Every act of kindness towards animals creates a more compassionate and responsible society.

Project UDAAN

Empowering women through skills, awareness, and financial independence.

When women are empowered, families thrive and communities become stronger.

Project PRAKRITI

Promoting environmental conservation through tree plantation and sustainable practices.

Every tree planted today helps create a healthier and greener future for generations to come.

Project VIKAS

Providing internships and professional training to students and young professionals.

Developing practical skills today prepares young people for successful careers tomorrow.

Social Impact

The initiatives of InAmigos Foundation have created a meaningful impact by improving education access, supporting communities, empowering women, protecting the environment, caring for animals, and preparing young people for career opportunities. Together, these projects contribute towards building stronger, healthier, and self-reliant communities.
